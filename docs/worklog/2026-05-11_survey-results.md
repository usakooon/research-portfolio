# 2026-05-11 調査結果

> **Summary (EN):** Results of the field surveys at both stations: interviews, pedestrian counts and an environment checklist. At Shin-Yurigaoka 13 elderly people were interviewed. The pedestrian-space Route A scored clearly higher than the roadside Route B on walkability (mean 4.25 vs 2.60), sense of safety (5.00 vs 4.40) and comfort (4.38 vs 3.60), and greenery and the absence of cars and bicycles were the reasons given most often. Counts were similar (159 vs 143 passers-by, about 37% apparently elderly). At Nagareyama-Otakanomori only 4 interviews were obtained, all rated 4–5, and the plaza-side Route B carried far more pedestrians than Route A (131 vs 20).
>
> **Phase:** 5. Second site & field survey (流山・現地調査) · **Tools/Data:** Field survey (pedestrian counts, interviews with 5-point ratings, environment checklist)
